## Information for Participants

### 📌 What’s this about?
Your organisation is exploring opportunities to evolve its technology platforms to better support speed, scalability, and alignment with 
business needs. As we look at ways to improve how our systems support the work we do, this short questionnaire helps us identify 
strengths, gaps, and opportunities across teams and technologies.This questionnaire is designed to help us understand 
where we are today — technically, culturally, and organisationally — and where we may want to focus our efforts to improve.

### 👥 Who should take part?
We’re gathering perspectives across four key groups:

- Engineering: on architecture, system maturity, team capabilities, and technical bottlenecks.
- Product: on delivery practices, team autonomy, and alignment with business goals.
- Business: on strategy, integration readiness, and understanding of the platform’s potential 
   -> Heads of ..., Commercial directors, Category Managers, BAs, Senior stakeholders from non-technical departments
- Leadership: on sponsorship, organisational alignment, and cultural support for change
   -> CxO, Heads of Product, Technology, or Digital, Board-level or VP-level sponsors

Each group has a unique perspective. Your answers will help paint a full picture of where we’re aligned
and where we may need to prepare more.

### 📝 How it works
The questionnaire takes about 10–15 minutes.
You’ll rate a series of statements using a simple 1–5 scale (from “Strongly Disagree” to “Strongly Agree”).
You can optionally add comments to provide examples or highlight blockers.

### 🧠 Why your input matters
By participating, you help us:

- Identify risks early (e.g. unclear ownership, technical debt, resistance to change)
- Highlight opportunities for improvement
- Build alignment across teams before the migration begins
- Tailor the transition plan based on real organisational readiness

### 📊 What happens next?
We’ll analyse the data and generate a report that:

- Summarises readiness across six domains
- Visualises alignment and gaps
- Provides clear, actionable recommendations

This isn’t a performance review — it’s a diagnostic tool to help us succeed together.
